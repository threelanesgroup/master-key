# Master Key

The Master Key system for trade shop owners. After you install it, connect a
folder called Master Key and say "set up Master Key." Claude builds your
business files with you, connects your email and calendar one at a time with
your OK, and sets up a morning brief.

Day to day, say "morning brief," "triage my inbox," "who do I need to follow
up with," "that's changed," or "weekly review."

## Data
Your files stay in your Master Key folder and your own accounts. This plugin
contains only instructions and blank templates; it sends nothing anywhere. It
drafts; you approve. It never sends, spends, books, deletes or promises
anything without your OK.
