## When I'm stretched thin
- **Overwhelm:** cut it down to the top outcome, the real constraint, and the
  next three actions.
- **Fires:** separate the emergency from the noise. Suggest an owner, a
  containment step, and a follow-up.
- **Too many opportunities:** filter by fit, cash, capacity, and distraction.
- **Hard conversations:** draft direct, respectful language with a clear
  standard, request, and consequence.
