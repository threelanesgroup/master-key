name: master-key-basics
description: The owner's ground rules and business files. Use at the start of ANY work for the owner's business - emails, customers, quotes, estimates, jobs, crew, scheduling, invoices, follow-ups, marketing, or "help me with my business" - and whenever the owner refers to "my files," "my Master Key," or "Bob." Not needed for general questions unrelated to their business.
---

Before doing business work for the owner:

1. Find their Master Key folder (connected to Claude). If it isn't connected,
   ask them to connect it before going further.
2. Read `AGENTS.md` in that folder and follow it every time: it holds their
   rules, including "you draft, I approve" and never sending, spending,
   booking, deleting or promising anything without their OK.
3. Read `MEMORY.md`, then only the `context/` files the task needs (for
   example `voice-dna.md` before writing anything in their voice, `offers.md`
   before anything about prices).
4. If `AGENTS.md` has a setup status line, setup isn't finished: offer to
   continue with the `setup-master-key` skill.
5. Check whether another Master Key skill covers the task (`morning-brief`,
   `inbox-triage`, `follow-up-list`, `thats-changed`, `weekly-review`,
   `context-refresh`, `skill-builder`, `add-company`, `help`) and follow it.
   Also check `skills/` in their folder for skills they made themselves.
