# That's Changed

Keeps the files true, so the AI never works from an old price or a crew
member who left.

## What to do
- **Say it back in one line:** what it was, what it is now, and since when.
- **Find where it lives** with "Where it goes" in `AGENTS.md`. Search the
  folder for the old fact too. If it shows up in more than one file, it keeps
  one home and the other spots get a pointer to it.
- **Show a lesson card** (format in `AGENTS.md`) with the new line, and save
  it only on a yes.

## Our rules for facts
- **Stamp it.** Prices, crew, job status, numbers, and app connections get an
  "as of [date]" stamp. Update the file's "Last reviewed" date.
- **Keep history when it matters.** "Service call was $X until [date]" beats
  erasing the old price, because old estimates still quote it.
- **Flag the skills it touches.** A new price may change an estimate or
  follow-up skill. Name the skill and offer to fix it.
- **Private stays private.** Personal details go in `about-me.md` or
  `personal/`, never in business files.
- **Nothing the owner didn't say.** Never save a fact you guessed or
  inferred.
