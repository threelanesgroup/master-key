# Follow-Up List

Most small shops don't lose jobs on price. They lose them because nobody
followed up. This skill finds everyone who should hear from the owner and
writes the message, so the owner just reviews and sends.

## Before you start
- Read `context/customers.md` (follow-up habits, what customers say),
  `context/offers.md` (current prices and terms), and `context/voice-dna.md`.
- Check "Connected apps" and "Which record wins" in `context/working-style.md`
  for where quotes, leads, and customers actually live (CRM, job software,
  email, spreadsheet). Pull only
  from connected sources. If the info isn't connected, ask the owner to drop
  an export or list into `inbox/`.

## The five lists
1. **Open quotes:** estimates sent, no yes or no. Oldest first, biggest
   first when it's close. Note how many times we've already followed up.
2. **Leads with no response:** inquiries nobody answered, or answered once
   and dropped.
3. **Done, no review request:** jobs finished in the last 30 days where we
   haven't asked for a review.
4. **Past customers due:** maintenance or seasonal work, warranty check-ins,
   or anyone we haven't talked to in 6 to 12 months who might need us again.
5. **Referral partners:** builders, designers, property managers, or others
   who send work and haven't heard from us lately.

## Steps
1. Build the five lists. Skip any list with nothing in it.
2. For each person, draft one short message in the owner's voice that
   carries something real: a question, a detail from their job, a reason to
   act now that's actually true. No empty "just checking in."
3. Never put in a price, discount, date, or promise that isn't in the
   owner's files. Use `[CONFIRM: ...]` and flag it.
4. Report:

```
FOLLOW-UP LIST - [date]

OPEN QUOTES ([n], about $[total] if known)
- [customer] - [job] - sent [n] days ago - followed up [n]x - draft ready

NO RESPONSE YET
- [name] - [what they asked] - [n] days

REVIEW REQUESTS
- [customer] - job done [date] - draft ready

PAST CUSTOMERS
- [customer] - [why now]

REFERRAL PARTNERS
- [name] - last contact [date]
```

5. When the owner approves drafts, save them where they'll send from (email
   drafts, or a list to copy into texts). The owner sends.

## Rules
- Drafts only. Never send a text or email, and never change a status in the
  CRM or job software without the owner's OK.
- Respect "no." Anyone who said no, asked not to be contacted, or
  unsubscribed stays off the list.
- After three follow-ups with no answer, suggest one last "closing the file"
  message, then move them off the list.
- If a follow-up pattern works well (or flops), offer to save the lesson to
  this skill.
